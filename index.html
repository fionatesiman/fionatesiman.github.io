TRIBUTE TO THE PIONEERS

Pixel art's charm was born out of intense hardware limitations. This spread celebrates:

  • Toru Iwatani (1980): Creator of Pac-Man, designing instantly identifiable shapes within an 8x8 dot matrix.
  • Masahiro Sakurai (1992): Creator of Kirby, utilizing simple geometric silhouettes to convey immense personality on the original monochrome Game Boy.
  • Shigeru Miyamoto (1981): Creator of Mario & Donkey Kong, giving Mario his iconic mustache and cap to bypass the impossibility of rendering mouth and hair movements in early arcade hardware.
INFOGRAPHIC BREAKDOWN

A SIMPLE GUIDE TO MAKING PIXEL ART

Designed as an engaging double-page foldout poster for readers to learn fundamental sprite techniques:

1
Canvas & Grid Setup

Starting with low resolutions (16x16, 32x32) to master proportion before scale.

2
Line Art & Outlines

Preventing "jaggies" by ensuring smooth step counts (2-2-2 or 1-1-1).

3
Flat Color Blocking

Restricting color ramps to 3–4 tones per material for sharp readability.

4
Shading & Dithering

Employing intentional light sources, edge highlights, and checkerboard textures.
